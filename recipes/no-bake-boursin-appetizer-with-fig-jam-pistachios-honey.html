No-Bake Boursin Appetizer with Fig Jam, Pistachios & Honey

No-bake, 4-ingredient appetizer that takes just a few minutes. Great for fall get-togethers, girls' night, football night, or weekend dinner. Exact quantities and steps weren't given in the caption; steps are inferred from the ingredient layering.

Ingredients

  • Boursin cheese
  • Fig jam
  • Pistachios (crushed)
  • Honey

Method

  1. Place the Boursin cheese on a serving plate.
  2. Top with fig jam.
  3. Sprinkle with crushed pistachios.
  4. Drizzle with honey and serve.
