Trader Joe's Salsa Verde Chicken & Rice Casserole

Serves 4–6

The post doesn't say how to put the casserole together, only "assemble and bake."

Original reel: https://www.instagram.com/reels/DdmypneuN09/

Ingredients

  • 1 package Trader Joe's Hardwood Smoked Pulled Chicken
  • 1 box (20 oz) Trader Joe's Spanish Style Rice (both pouches)
  • 1 can Trader Joe's Organic Lentil Vegetable Soup
  • ½ jar Trader Joe's Salsa Verde (from a 12 oz jar)
  • ½ bag Trader Joe's Finely Shredded Mexican Style Cheese Blend (from a 6 oz bag)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Assemble the pulled chicken, Spanish style rice, lentil vegetable soup, salsa verde and shredded cheese in a baking dish.
  3. Bake for 30 minutes at 375°F.
