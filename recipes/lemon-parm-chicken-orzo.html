Lemon Parm Chicken Orzo

Serves 7

Takes about 20 minutes. Good for meal prep or a family dinner. The creator scaled it down to 4 servings, but the quantities here are for 7. A commenter suggested Greek yogurt instead of cream cheese.

Original reel: https://www.instagram.com/p/DeChDcHicKs/

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs (907g) Chicken breast, raw & skinless (diced)
  • 2.5 cups (400g) Orzo pasta, dry
  • 6 cups (1,440 mL) Chicken broth
  • 3 tsp (15g) Minced garlic
  • 1 Large shallot (finely diced)
  • 3 cups (360g) Frozen peas & carrots
  • 1 block (8 oz / 226g) Reduced-fat cream cheese
  • 2 cups (200g) Parmesan cheese
  • 2 lemons Lemon juice (juiced)
  • 2 lemons Lemon zest (zested)
  • 1 tsp Paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Black pepper

Method

  1. Cook diced chicken in a large skillet over medium-high heat with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper for 5–7 minutes.
  2. Add shallot and garlic and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Stir in dry orzo and toast for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Add chicken broth, peas, and carrots. Cover and simmer for 10–12 minutes until orzo is tender.
  5. Stir in cream cheese, parmesan, lemon zest, and lemon juice until creamy.
  6. Divide into 7 equal servings and store or serve.
