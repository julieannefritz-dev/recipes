Kati's Edamame Salad

Serves 4–6

Good made ahead because the flavors have more time to come together. In the video she makes a double batch (4 packages of edamame, one bowl for her house and one for her parents' house) and also adds chopped cucumbers, which aren't in the written recipe. She has an e-book of recipes inspired by Trader Joe's ingredients, linked in her bio's Linktree.

Original reel: https://www.facebook.com/reel/2099682007325322

Ingredients

  • 2 (9 oz.) packages Trader Joe's Shelled Edamame (Shelled Soybeans with Sea Salt) (ready-to-use, from the produce section)
  • ¼ cup fresh dill (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley (finely chopped)
  • ½ cup Trader Joe's Authentic Greek Feta (crumbled; feta brand seen in video)
  • ¼ cup red onion (finely chopped)
  • cucumbers (chopped; seen in video, not in written recipe)
  • ½ cup extra virgin olive oil (for vinaigrette)
  • 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar (for vinaigrette)
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard (for vinaigrette)
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup (for vinaigrette)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder (for vinaigrette)
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder (for vinaigrette)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper (freshly cracked, for vinaigrette)
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt (or to taste, for vinaigrette)

Method

  1. Add the ready-to-use shelled edamame to a large bowl. Add the dill, parsley, feta, and red onion.
  2. Add all of the vinaigrette ingredients to a mason jar and blend with a small immersion-style blender until smooth. Or cover the jar tightly and shake well until combined.
  3. Pour your desired amount of Maple Dijon Vinaigrette over the salad and toss until everything is evenly coated.
  4. Serve right away or refrigerate until ready to enjoy.
