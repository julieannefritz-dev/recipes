No-Bake Boursin Fig Appetizer

No-bake, 4 ingredients, takes just a few minutes. Great for fall get-togethers, girls' night, football night or weekend dinner. Quantities and exact method not specified in the post; steps inferred from ingredient list.

Ingredients

  • Boursin cheese
  • Fig jam
  • Pistachios (crushed)
  • Honey

Method

  1. Place the Boursin cheese on a serving plate.
  2. Spread fig jam over the top.
  3. Sprinkle with crushed pistachios.
  4. Drizzle with honey and serve.
