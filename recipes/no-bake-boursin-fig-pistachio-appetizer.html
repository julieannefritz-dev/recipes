No-Bake Boursin Fig Pistachio Appetizer

No-bake, 4 ingredients, ready in a few minutes. Good for a fall get-together, girls' night, football night or weekend dinner. The post didn't give quantities or exact steps, so the method is inferred from the ingredient list.

Ingredients

  • Boursin cheese
  • Fig jam
  • Pistachios (crushed)
  • Honey

Method

  1. Place the Boursin cheese on a serving plate.
  2. Top with fig jam.
  3. Sprinkle with crushed pistachios.
  4. Drizzle with honey and serve.
