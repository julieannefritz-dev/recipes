Creamy Chicken Enchilada Rice Bake

Serves 6

Great easy weeknight dinner / meal prep. Note: the post text also contained unrelated recipes from other accounts (sweet potato brunch bowls, hot honey chicken salad, rice paper bagel), which were excluded.

Original reel: https://www.instagram.com/reels/DdhLyGvtrme/

Ingredients

  • 1½ lbs chicken breast (cooked and shredded)
  • 1 cup rice (uncooked measure, then cooked)
  • 1 can black beans (drained + rinsed)
  • 1 cup corn
  • ~15 oz red enchilada sauce
  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • salt + pepper (to taste)
  • 1½ cups shredded Mexican cheese (divided: 1 cup mixed in, ½ cup on top)
  • ½ cup water (for cooking chicken in Instant Pot, plus a pinch of salt)

Method

  1. Cook chicken in the Instant Pot with ½ cup water and a pinch of salt on high pressure for 10 minutes, natural release for 5 minutes, then shred.
  2. Mix enchilada sauce, Greek yogurt, seasonings, shredded chicken, cooked rice, black beans, corn, and 1 cup cheese.
  3. Spread into a greased 9x13 dish and top with the remaining cheese.
  4. Cover and bake at 375° for 20 minutes.
  5. Uncover and bake another 8–10 minutes until hot and bubbly.
  6. Let sit 5–10 minutes, then divide into 6 servings.
