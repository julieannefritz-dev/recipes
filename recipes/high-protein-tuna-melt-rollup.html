High Protein Tuna Melt Rollup

Serves 2

Each serving has more than 50g of protein. Shave the zucchini super thin, and keep an eye on the baking time because ovens vary. The tuna used (Safe Catch) has 40g of protein and 150 calories per can. Comment "BREAD" on the post to get the creator's free low-carb bread ebook.

Original reel: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd7l7j9hn8B/

Ingredients

  • 1 large zucchini (thinly shaved with a mandolin)
  • 2 servings shredded cheese (1 serving under the zucchini, 1 on top)
  • garlic powder (to season)
  • 2 cans canned tuna (Safe Catch used in the post)
  • 2 hardboiled eggs
  • 2 scallions (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp fresh dill (finely chopped)
  • 1/4 cup red onion (finely diced)
  • 1/4 cup dill pickles (finely diced (the caption says '14 cup', most likely a typo for 1/4))
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tbsp dijon mustard
  • salt (to taste)
  • pepper (to taste)
  • lemon juice (a bit)

Toppings

  • tomatoes
  • jalapeño

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 400F. Shave 1 large zucchini very thin with a mandolin. Getting the slices super thin is key.
  2. Pat the slices dry. Line an oven-safe tray with parchment paper and sprinkle a thin layer (1 serving) of shredded cheese on the paper. Lay the zucchini slices on top, slightly overlapping, to form a rectangle. Add another serving of cheese on top and season with garlic powder.
  3. Bake for 18-20 minutes, until the edges are golden and the cheese is crispy. Ovens vary, so watch it closely.
  4. Make the tuna salad: mix the tuna with the hardboiled eggs, scallions, dill, red onion, pickles, mayo, dijon, salt, pepper and lemon juice. Taste and adjust.
  5. Let the zucchini sheet cool slightly. Spread the tuna salad over it and top with tomatoes and jalapeño.
  6. Roll it up tightly from the bottom, pressing gently as you go to keep the filling compact. Cut it in the middle with a sharp knife and serve.
