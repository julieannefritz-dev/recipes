Fat Loss Broccoli Protein Soup

Serves 1

Macros are for the soup only, before toppings. For a more soupy consistency add more liquid; otherwise it's thick like a smoothie bowl or dip. Rated 9.1/10.

Original reel: https://www.instagram.com/reels/Dd9YJKhij-2/

Ingredients

  • 400g Broccoli (approx. 4 cups chopped)
  • 150g 0% Greek yogurt (approx. 2/3 cup)
  • 200ml Chicken broth (approx. 3/4 cup)
  • 25g Yellow onion (approx. 2 tbsp diced)
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1–2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • Garlic powder (to taste)
  • Salt + black pepper (to taste)

Toppings

  • Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 15g (approx. 2 tbsp)
  • Chili flakes — to taste
  • Optional alternatives: cheddar, avocado, etc.

Method

  1. Simmer the broccoli, yellow onion, and chicken broth covered in a pot until very soft.
  2. Add seasonings and blend until smooth.
  3. Blend or stir in the Greek yogurt until creamy and thick.
  4. Pour into a bowl, top with grated Parmigiano Reggiano and chili flakes, and serve warm.
