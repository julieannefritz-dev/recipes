Frozen Yogurt Bark

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • handful blueberries
  • sliced almonds
  • pinch cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix the Greek yogurt, honey and cinnamon, then spread on a tray.
  2. Top with blueberries and sliced almonds.
  3. Freeze for 2 hours.
  4. Snap into pieces.
